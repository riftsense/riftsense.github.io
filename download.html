What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC.
  • Overwolf, the free platform RiftSense runs on. If you don't have it, installing RiftSense installs it too.
  • League of Legends.

How it works

  1. Install RiftSense from the Overwolf app store.
  2. Start League of Legends: RiftSense starts with the game.
  3. In game you get the timers on the minimap and the widget; after the game, your match history opens with the game you just played.

Free

RiftSense is free. It shows ads provided by Overwolf in its main window, never over the game.
